Kun Assistant 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-11-10

  1. 存取範圍：僅限擁有者本人授權之 Google 帳戶的 Gmail、Google 日曆與 Google Tasks。
  2. 用途：為擁有者本人整理與搜尋信件、查詢與提醒行程及待辦，不作其他用途。
  3. 處理與傳送：資料由擁有者使用之 AI 服務（Anthropic Claude）處理，處理結果經 Telegram 傳送給擁有者本人。
  4. 儲存：Google 授權憑證只存於擁有者本人的電腦，不上傳至其他伺服器。
  5. 分享：不出售、不分享任何資料給第三方，也不用於廣告。
  6. 撤銷：可隨時於 Google 帳戶「安全性 > 第三方存取權」撤銷 Kun Assistant 之授權。

Privacy Policy (English)

Kun Assistant only accesses the Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Tasks of Google accounts its owner has personally authorized, solely to organize mail and remind the owner of schedules and to-dos. Data is processed by the AI service the owner uses (Anthropic Claude) and the results are delivered to the owner via Telegram. OAuth credentials are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account's third-party access settings.
