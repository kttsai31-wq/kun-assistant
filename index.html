Kun Assistant

Kun Assistant 是擁有者個人自用的助理程式，經擁有者本人以 Google 帳戶授權後，讀取擁有者本人的 Gmail、Google 日曆與 Google Tasks，用於整理信件、查詢與提醒行程及待辦。本程式不對外提供服務，也不開放他人使用。

Kun Assistant is a personal assistant used only by its owner. With the owner's own Google authorization, it reads the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Tasks to organize mail and remind the owner of schedules and to-dos. It is not offered to anyone else.
